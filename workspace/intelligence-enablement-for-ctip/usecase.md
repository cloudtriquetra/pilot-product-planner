# Intelligence enablement for CTIP

## Project Overview
- **Description:** To increase the effectiveness of threat information post-processing augmented with AI using the local LLM as a force multiplier to model the threat intelligence in the CTIP at higher scale and in a more consistent way. CTIP plays the role of a single source of of threat intelligence for the Bank, the initiative will help to augment extraction of most relevant threat information from the unstructured information collected by Cyber Intelligence Centre.
- **Target Platforms:** web
